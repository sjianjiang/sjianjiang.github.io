Profile

Research at the intersection of networks, imaging, and prediction.

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Georgia and a Graduate Research Assistant in the NeuraConnect Lab. My work focuses on turning complex neuroimaging signals into interpretable computational models for clinical and translational neuroscience.

Research Focus

Building connectome models that explain as well as predict.

Connectomics

Structural-functional multiplex networks

Developed multilayer connectome models using convolutional and transformer-based neural network approaches for traumatic brain injury diagnosis and clinical outcome prediction.

Neuroimaging

Multimodal MRI pipelines

Integrated fMRI and dMRI features into connectome matrices using CONN Toolbox, DSI Studio, Python, and MATLAB for downstream machine learning analysis.

Interpretability

Saliency and attribution analysis

Performed input saliency, true-class attribution, and diagonal-importance checks to identify influential matrix features and strengthen model quality control.

Prediction

Seizure-pattern classification

Categorized ictal-period onset patterns from primate EEG recordings and applied machine learning methods to compare seizure onset types and reduce artifacts.
